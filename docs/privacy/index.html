Duebloom

Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Duebloom keeps your subscriptions on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see what you track.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter stays on your device: subscriptions, prices, dates, notes, account emails, reminder settings and your onboarding choices. Duebloom also saves service logos it has downloaded and the latest service catalogue there.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) may include this data, as they do for any app. Backup and CSV files you export from Settings aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

What leaves your iPhone

Duebloom makes only these requests. None of them include your subscriptions, prices, notes or emails.

  • Service logos. With logos on, Duebloom asks Logo.dev for a service's logo by its website (for example netflix.com). Logo.dev sees that website and your IP address. If you give a subscription your own image link, that address is asked directly. You can turn logos off in Settings → Privacy → Show service logos.
  • The service catalogue. At most once a day, Duebloom downloads the public list of services, prices and cancellation pages from GitHub Pages. GitHub sees only your IP address. This file holds no data about you.
  • Purchases and notifications. Buying or restoring Duebloom Unlimited goes through Apple's App Store. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Duebloom doesn't run a server for either.

What we collect

Nothing. Duebloom has no server of its own and collects no personal data, usage data or diagnostics. Logo.dev and GitHub have their own privacy policies for the requests described above.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes every subscription from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything Duebloom stored.

Children

Duebloom doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
