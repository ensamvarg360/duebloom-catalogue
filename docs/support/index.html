Common questions

Does Duebloom cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Duebloom never signs in to your accounts. It reminds you before a trial ends or a charge renews, and links to the provider's own cancellation page where we know it.

I'm not getting nudges.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Duebloom, and that at least one rule is on in Duebloom's Settings. "Send me a test nudge" in Settings confirms they arrive. Nudges go out at your chosen Nudge time.

A price or cancel link is wrong.

The catalogue is updated without an app update. Email us or report it on GitHub, and the app picks up the fix within a day.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

An iPhone backup or transfer brings Duebloom's data along. You can also use Save a backup in Settings → Your data, then Restore from a backup on the new phone.

I bought Unlimited but it isn't showing.

Open Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
